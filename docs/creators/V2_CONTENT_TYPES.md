# New website content (v2)

The new website keeps more of its words in Sanity, so you can change them without a developer. This guide covers the **v2** workspace. The current website still uses the **production** workspace, and nothing there changes until the new site launches.

## Where to find it

In Sanity Studio, use the workspace menu at the top left and choose **The Source of Hope (v2)**. It edits the `staging` dataset while we build. At launch it moves to `production-v2`.

## Things every document has in common

- **English is required.** Each text field has an English box. Other languages are added later as translations, so leave them empty.
- **Every image needs alt text and a consent check.** Write one sentence saying what the photo shows (leave out "image of"). Tick **Consent confirmed** only when everyone recognizable agreed to the photo being used. You can't publish without both. Tick **Decorative only** for purely decorative images; they then need no alt text.
- **Use the official names.** Programs, titles and the phone number must match the glossary in **Site settings → Glossary**.

## One-off pages and settings

These open directly from the top of the menu. There is only ever one of each.

| Item | What it controls |
|---|---|
| Site settings | Phone `(469) 969-0244`, email, address, office hours, the impact numbers (each with an "as of" date), meals per dollar, and the glossary |
| Our Story | Mission, vision, history, founders (name, title, photo, short bio), and "How we work" |
| Planned giving | The introduction, ways to give, and the legal name used in wills |
| Sponsor tiers | The Community Sponsor tiers: name, monthly amount and benefits. A tier's **Key** must never change once the tier is live |

## Content lists

| Type | What it is |
|---|---|
| Programs | The five program pages. Each has a name, one line, a hero image, "What it is", "Who it serves", "When and where", a gallery (up to 12 photos), questions and answers, and links to program forms. Only one document per program |
| Get Help services | What people can get help with: food, health care, tutoring and school, outdoor programs. Write at a 6th–8th grade reading level. **How to start** is one choice: call, apply online (add the link), just come, or email or call |
| Questions and answers | One question and answer each, tagged with a topic. Add them to a program to show them on its page |
| Recurring programs | The "Every month" list on the Events page |
| Team | Groups, and members with title, bios and photo |
| News & Media | Stories and updates, and newsletter issues |
| Store | Products and checkout providers |

## What isn't here anymore

**Events** move to the new Staff Portal, which publishes each event to the website, Eventbrite and Facebook from one form. Upcoming events are copied over before launch.

**Buttons, menus and form messages** stay with the developers, so every page uses the same words for the same action.
