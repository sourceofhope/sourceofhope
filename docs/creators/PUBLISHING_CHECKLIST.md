# Publishing Checklist

Content you publish in Sanity Studio appears on the website within about **30 seconds**, so it's important that it is up-to-date, on-theme, appropriate, and helps spread hope.

Before clicking **Publish**, confirm:

- Title and name spelling are correct
- Required fields are filled in
- Slug is correct (and unchanged, if the item was already live)
- Image is resized and compressed (see `/docs/creators/MEDIA_FORMAT_RULES.md`)
- Alt text is filled in
- Links open the right page
- Dates and times are correct, in Central time
- Prices are correct (for products, this is what customers pay)
- No spelling errors
- Content matches the brand tone (see `/docs/organizers/BRAND_CONTENT_RULES.md`)

After publishing:

- Wait about 30 seconds, then open the page on the website and check it looks right.

This prevents most common issues.
