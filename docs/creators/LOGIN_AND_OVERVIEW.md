# CMS Login and Overview

This guide is for digital content creators. You do not need access to the codebase.

All of your work happens **inside the CMS**.

---

## What You Are Responsible For

You can safely:

- Create new pages and posts
- Edit text and images
- Upload media
- Publish and schedule content
- Update existing content

You should never attempt to:

- Change navigation
- Change page layouts
- Modify forms
- Edit URLs
- Edit anything outside the CMS

If you need those changes, contact a developer.

---

## Logging Into the CMS

1. Visit the CMS (cms.thesourceofhope.org)
2. Enter your username and password provided by the administrator.
3. You will land on the CMS dashboard.

From here, you will primarily use:

- Pages
- Posts
- Media Library
- Post Type(s)
