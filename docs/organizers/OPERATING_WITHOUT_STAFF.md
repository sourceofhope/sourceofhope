# Operating the Website Without Staff or Volunteers

As a non-profit, there may be times when no volunteers or content creators are available.

You can still keep the website updated yourself from Sanity Studio by focusing only on these tasks:

- Updating featured and recurring events
- Posting publications (news, articles, press links)
- Adding newsletter issues
- Updating team members
- Updating store products and prices
- Replacing outdated images

All of this can be done without technical knowledge. Start with `/docs/creators/LOGIN_AND_OVERVIEW.md`.

Wording on the pages themselves (for example the About and Programs pages) is part of the website code. Changing it requires a developer; see `/docs/organizers/WHAT_NEEDS_DEVELOPER.md`.

---

## Minimum Monthly Maintenance

At least once per month:

- Check the Events page: past featured events move to "Recently completed". Move recurring programs to their next date, and check that each event asks for the right volunteer waiver.
- Check the home page "Latest Updates" and the Media page for recent publications.
- Check the store: products, prices, and photos are current.
- Check the Team page for people who have joined or left.
- Check the contact information in the website footer and on the Connect page (changes go to a developer).
- Ensure images are appropriate and load quickly.

This keeps the website current and trustworthy.
