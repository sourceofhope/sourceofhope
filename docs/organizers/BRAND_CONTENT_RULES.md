# Brand and Content Rules

The website represents the organization publicly. Content should always follow these principles.

---

## Tone

- Professional
- Community-focused
- Hopeful and service-oriented
- Clear and easy to read

Avoid overly casual language or cluttered text.

---

## Images and Media

- Use real photos from events and programs when possible
- Follow the media rules in `/docs/content-creators/MEDIA_IMAGE_RULES.md`
- Avoid stock images unless necessary
- Avoid using AI-generated media

---

## Accuracy

Before publishing:

- Verify dates, times, and locations
- Verify links work
- Confirm program names are correct
- Confirm contact information is current
