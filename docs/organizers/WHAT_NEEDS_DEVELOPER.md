# What Requires a Developer

Sanity Studio only controls a few kinds of content (see `/docs/organizers/WHAT_YOU_CAN_CHANGE.md`). Everything else is part of the website code and needs a developer.

---

## Page Wording and Images

Most text and images on the website are written into the code, including:

- The home page
- The About page
- The Programs page and each program page (Serving & Sharing Hope, Education for Hope, Wellness of Hope, Hope for the Outdoors, Hope Beyond Borders)
- The Press, Podcast, and Awards pages
- The Planned Giving page
- The Memberships page, including plan names, descriptions, and prices
- The Donate page
- The Connect page (contact details, hours, map)
- The footer (email, phone, address, social links)

---

## Structure and Navigation

- Adding, removing, or renaming items in the top navigation or footer
- Adding a new page or a new type of page layout
- Redirecting old web addresses to new ones
- The order of team groups on the Team page
- Connecting new Studio fields or content types to the website

---

## Money and Forms

- Store shipping options, shipping costs, sales tax rate, or the processing fee
- Membership plan prices (they are set in two places in the code and must match)
- Changes to checkout, donation, or membership behavior
- Making Google Pay or Apple Pay work
- A new form, or changes to where form messages are sent

---

## Other

- Turning on a site-wide banner (the Banner Event content type is not currently connected to the website)
- Adding or replacing the volunteer agreement PDFs
- Changing the home page video or any other video on the site
- Fixing broken layouts
- Performance or speed issues
- The website being down, or a bad update

These changes involve the website code, its settings, or its hosting.
