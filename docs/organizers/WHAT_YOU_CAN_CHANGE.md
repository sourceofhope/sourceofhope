# What You Can Safely Change

As an organizer or owner, you can manage most of the website without needing a developer, as long as you stay inside the CMS.

You can safely:

- Edit text on pages
- Create new pages and posts
- Upload and replace images
- Publish news, events, and updates
- Update staff, programs, and event information
- Embed videos from YouTube or Vimeo
- Correct spelling or outdated information

These changes do not affect the structure of the website.

---

## What You Must Not Change

Do not attempt to change:

- Navigation menus
- Page layout or design
- Forms or contact sections
- URLs or page slugs
- Payment or donation pages
- Anything outside the CMS

These require developer assistance.
