# What You Can Safely Change

As an organizer or owner, you can manage the website's changing content without a developer, as long as you stay inside Sanity Studio.

---

## What the Studio Controls

You can safely:

- Add, edit, and remove **team members** and **team groups** (Team page and profile pages)
- Add and update **featured events** and **recurring events** (Events page)
- Post **publications** — news, articles, and press links (home page "Latest Updates" and the Media page)
- Add **newsletter** issues (Media page)
- Add and update **store products**, including their **prices**, descriptions, and photos
- Turn **checkout providers** (Stripe, PayPal express buttons) on or off
- Upload and replace images for all of the above
- Link to videos hosted on YouTube or Vimeo
- Correct spelling or outdated information in any of the above

These changes do not affect the structure of the website, and they appear within about 30 seconds of publishing.

How-to guides: `/docs/creators/CREATE_EDIT_DELETE_CONTENT.md` and `/docs/creators/CONTENT_TYPES_REFERENCE.md`.

---

## What You Must Not Change

Do not attempt to change:

- Navigation menus
- Page layout or design
- Page wording that is not in the Studio
- Forms or contact sections
- Web addresses of existing pages, or the slugs of live team members and products
- Payment, donation, or membership pages
- Anything outside the Studio

These require developer assistance. See `/docs/organizers/WHAT_NEEDS_DEVELOPER.md`.
